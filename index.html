Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some common questions about our products and orders.

How can I place an order?

You can place an enquiry by sending us an email at Housenibble@gmail.com. Tell us what you'd like to order, the quantity and your preferred date.

Do you accept orders for events?

Yes. We welcome orders for birthdays, weddings, parties, corporate events and other special occasions. Please contact us early so we can discuss your needs.

Can I request a customised cake?

Yes. For customised cakes, please send us your preferred design, size, flavour and event date. We'll respond with the available options.

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend placing event and customised orders as early as possible. Availability may depend on the size and details of your order.

How do I contact Housenibble?

You can reach us anytime by email at Housenibble@gmail.com.
